Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Our application operates primarily client-side. We do not collect or store personal user passwords, emails, or financial credentials on external remote servers.

2. LocalStorage Data Usage

We store your game tokens balance, guest profile identifier, and match betting history locally on your device via browser LocalStorage. This data stays entirely on your browser and can be cleared by you at any time.

3. Third-Party Advertising & AdSense

We may use third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to display advertisements or reward simulations. These partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve relevant ads based on general web interaction.

4. Cookies Policy

The application itself uses minimal cookies or local tokens solely to maintain persistent game state and user session continuity.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy, you may reach out through the application support channels.
